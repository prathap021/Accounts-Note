Delete your Accounts Note account

Accounts Note, by Prathap A · Last updated: 8 November 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Email selflearner.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Accounts Note account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you sign in with, or include that address, so we can find your account.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the account and its data within 30 days.

Want to remove your data but keep your account? In the app, open Settings → Clear all data. It deletes every entry at once, from your phone and the cloud.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, profile photo and sign-in.
  • Your ledger: all income and expense entries, categories, budgets, recurring entries, accounts and transfers, credit cards (name and last four digits) and receipt photos.
  • Your settings and usage counts stored with your account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records: subscription purchases are recorded by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies. To stop being charged, cancel the subscription in the Play Store or App Store; deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics) contain no ledger data and are deleted automatically by Google within 90 days (crash reports) and 14 months (statistics).
  • Backup files you saved to your phone, email or cloud drive are in your hands and are not affected; delete them yourself if you want them gone.

Nothing else is kept. Questions: selflearner.dev@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
